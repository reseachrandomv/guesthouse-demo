Tharihiyaa — “star shade”

Not a resort. A home on the island, with the doors left open.

We're a family-run guesthouse on a local island, so you'll eat what we eat, swim where we swim and leave knowing the names of your neighbours. Breakfast is mas huni and fresh roshi. Dinner happens when the boats come in.
